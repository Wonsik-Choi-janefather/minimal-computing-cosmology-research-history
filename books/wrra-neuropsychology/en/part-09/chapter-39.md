# Chapter 39 Applications in Education, Work, Health, and Technology Environments

### 1 Scene and question

Schools examine retention and transfer; workplaces examine performance and cooperation; health management examines sustained behavior; ergonomics studies person–device interaction. Similar failures may arise from limited knowledge, interface problems, time constraints, or authority restrictions.

Chapter question: How should designs reducing execution burdens across people and environments be evaluated?

### 2 Starting points in established research

#### 2.1 Retention and transfer in education

Practice accuracy, delayed memory, and transfer to new problems are different achievements. Original research has examined retrieval tests' contribution to long-term retention. (Roediger and Karpicke, 2006 [R32](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x))

#### 2.2 Workplace and organizational design

Errors require examining knowledge, unclear instructions, authority, and information transmission as alternative causes.

#### 2.3 Health behavior and everyday technology

Intentions may not produce sustained actions without time, place, and support. (Ajzen, 1991 [R47](https://doi.org/10.1016/0749-5978(91)90020-T))

#### 2.4 An experiment separating immediate performance from delayed memory

Roediger and Karpicke had students restudy prose or take recall tests without feedback and then compared memory. Repeated study was advantageous after 5 minutes, while prior retrieval testing showed advantages after 2 days and 1 week. Familiarity during study and later retention were therefore different indicators. (Roediger and Karpicke, 2006 [R32](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1467-9280.2006.01693.x))

Scope of evidence: findings concern the materials and testing conditions used. WRRA distinguishes current assistance from retained structure without assuming identical effect sizes across every subject and learner.

### 4 WRRA-specific transformation

Transformation specification: design where burdens are placed across the person and environment.

WRRA asks what must be remembered, what can be recorded externally, and which transmission structures can be reused. Good information placement reducing errors can be compared with designs demanding more personal memory. Minimal computation is assessed within an explicit combined person–environment boundary.

#### 4.1 Retention and transfer in education — WRRA interpretation

Practice-provided information differs from independently maintained residue. Under matched study time and task conditions, structures and outcomes can be compared.

#### 4.2 Workplace and organizational design — WRRA interpretation

Inputs, staff relationships and records, resources, and permission boundaries are specified. Removing checks needed in exceptional cases requires reassessing preserved function. Safety-critical redundancy may be part of minimum cost.

#### 4.3 Health behavior and everyday technology — WRRA interpretation

Intention differs from execution conditions. Reminder and recording-tool effects differ from changed personal habits. Recommending a health intervention requires its own evidence; plausible structure alone does not establish effectiveness.

### 6 Falsification conditions and interpretive limits

Falsification condition: cost reduction alone does not establish success if functional or safety criteria fail.

Improved educational or work performance does not justify every form of surveillance. Psychological data collection requires explicit purposes, authority, and uses. Other applications, including forensic and sports psychology, retain their own standards and ethics. This manuscript organizes possibilities without separately establishing empirical application effects.

#### 6.1 Extending to forensic, sports, and environmental fields

Memory and judgment accuracy in forensic psychology, skill and pressure in sports, and space–behavior relationships in environmental psychology can be investigated. Shared grammar preserves each field's data and criteria. Concrete achievements require disclosure of variables actually connected and outputs produced, with failures and boundaries retained for independent review.
