# WRRA Neuropsychology

**Exploring WRRA's Potential for Integration Across Disciplines**

Wonsik Choi · Core-linked bilingual edition v0.9 · 22 October 2026

This book examines the explanatory and computational contributions WRRA may make to connecting different disciplines. Psychology and neuroscience provide the first case: accounts of experience and behavior, the nervous system and the body are connected through a common structure.

Starting from the evidence in each field, readers can identify the connections actually made within WRRA, their outputs, and the questions still requiring investigation. The central question is what these connections add to understanding and computing phenomena.
