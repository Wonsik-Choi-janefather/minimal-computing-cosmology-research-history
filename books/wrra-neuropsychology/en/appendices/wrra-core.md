# Appendix A Standard Explanation of WRRA 1.0

This explanation preserves the definitions in reference W1 and draws on W2's introduction. Neuropsychological statements are this book's applications, not changes to Core 1.0 itself.

## A1 WRRA's name and starting point

WRRA means Wonsik Reality-Renderer Architecture. This account adapts the frozen Core 1.0 and existing book explanations for neuropsychology readers. It begins by distinguishing information, relationships and rules, present state, execution, and observation in complex phenomena. Particular cosmological equations and channel counts are not compulsory in every application.

## A2 Minimal computation

Minimal computation uses the states and processing needed to meet declared function, expression, stability, or survival criteria. It does not mean unconditionally computing less. Removing required distinctions until function fails is deficiency; necessary margins and redundancy may belong to minimum cost. Costs and preserved criteria must be specified first.

## A3 Common carrier

A common carrier is a reusable transmission or updating structure shared by distinguishable channels. Shared infrastructure does not make information owners, paths, or purposes identical. Networks reused by several functions are candidates in neuropsychology. Until sharing is demonstrated, these remain correspondence hypotheses, not predetermined neurotransmitters or brain regions.

## A4 Phenotype and observation

Internal information is not itself an observation. Present state becomes phenotype through execution and rendering rules under boundary conditions. An observable is the value obtained by reading that phenotype through a particular device and procedure. Distinctions among neural activity, behavior, and experience reports apply this principle. Even if phenotype and observable are implemented together, the connecting measurement procedure remains necessary.

## A5 The full execution structure

Core 1.0's common structure comprises SOURCE, RELATION and LAW, STATE and RESIDUE, BOUNDARY, COMMON CARRIER, UPDATE, RENDERER, PHENOTYPE, and OBSERVABLE, RECORD, and LEDGER. These are not necessarily neural-processing stages traversed once in a line. Applications specify feedback, parallel processing, and repeated updates. What corresponds to each element is central.

## A6 Fixed present and residue

For a past influence to act now, its effects must remain in present state, residue, or accessible records. A model may read past records if their present location and access paths are specified. Residue includes learned skills, familiarity, trust, and adaptation as well as injury or negative experiences. Persistent benefits and burdens are measured separately.

## A7 Relationships and boundaries

Relationships specify how elements influence others under rules. Boundaries specify system interiors and exteriors and permitted or blocked flows. Physical bodily boundaries, task action restrictions, and social authority have different implementation levels. Constraints are not all collapsed into one boundary variable. System scope is fixed to avoid counting external support as internal capacity.

## A8 Ownership and record ledgers

Equal numbers represent different variables when their owners differ. Ownership identifies whose information, resources, memory, and outputs are represented. Personal reports, another's estimates, neural measurements, and behavior records do not substitute for each other. Ledgers record values, units, times, sources, transformations, and outcomes. Accounting for physical conserved quantities differs from documenting measurement responsibility.

## A9 Interpretation, computation, prediction, and control

Interpretation reconstructs or explains targets using present or recorded information. Computation derives outcomes from specified inputs and rules. After a model is set using verified values, its derivation for unmeasured targets or conditions counts as that model's prediction. Control selects actions or interventions changing later states and function. Reproduction of known results counts as consistency and explanation; absence of new numbers does not remove its meaning.

## A10 Fail-closed claims and separation of applications

When inputs, rules, errors, or validation are insufficient, only completed work receives a verdict and the remainder stays open. Failure of an application differs from abandoning the entire Core. Revising neuropsychological coefficients or state definitions changes a domain application, not automatically Core 1.0. Independent prediction is not required for every explanation. Each achievement is judged after presenting validation inputs, WRRA-specific transformation, outputs, and falsification conditions.

This traces definitions into an application specification, executed outputs, and the verdict on what has actually been connected. The DOI identifies an archival source; it is not a peer-review or empirical-validation certificate.

Prediction terminology: this book records an unmeasured-condition output of a fixed model as that model's conditional prediction. Core §2.3 additionally specifies prediction performance through reduced loss or uncertainty for a future target. Those performance targets, comparison criteria, and loss or uncertainty measures must be specified separately when tested. This distinction does not make independent prediction a prerequisite for explanatory value or originality.
