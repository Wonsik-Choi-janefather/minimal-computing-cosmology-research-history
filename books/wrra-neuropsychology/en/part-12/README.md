# Part 12 Questions Extending from Neuropsychology to Consciousness

Part 11 connects neuropsychology with consciousness research; Part 12 addresses questions extending from that starting point. Animal and artificial-system consciousness connect with comparative cognition and computational consciousness research. Cosmological inevitability, souls, and reincarnation require separating testable subclaims from philosophical interpretation. This part explores WRRA's capacity for cross-disciplinary connection rather than declaring these topics established neuropsychological knowledge.

If consciousness involves experience, one can also ask how time continues within it, what conditions allow such structures in the universe, and what memories and organization remain beyond an individual. These questions are examined as distinct claims. Philosophical interpretations, published findings, WRRA application specifications, and unestablished hypotheses do not receive one interchangeable verdict. Renaming an existing theory in WRRA terms is also distinguished from executing an actual transformation connecting them.
