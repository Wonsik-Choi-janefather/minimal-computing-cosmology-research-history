# Part 11 The Structure and Assessment of Consciousness — Extending the Application to WRRA Core 2.1

Chapter 12 introduced human consciousness, nonconscious processing, sleep, and dreams. Here the questions broaden. What must be preserved if the material implementing consciousness changes? How does a present experience differ from the organization enabling it, and what organization continues into the next execution? Part 11 begins with concepts and experiments in consciousness research, then proposes a domain application mapping those distinctions to Core 2.1 structure, state, retained effects, resources, and provenance. The Core itself does not determine whether consciousness is present.
