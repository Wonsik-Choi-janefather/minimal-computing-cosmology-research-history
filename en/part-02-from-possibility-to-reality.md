# Chapter 9 Is it necessary to realize all possibilities at once?

Opening up the space of possible universes does not mean that all of those possibilities must become physical realities at the same time.Mathematics can allow for numerous states, but the observer always experiences events within a reality with some persistence and locality.The problem of selection and compression remains between the set of possibilities and the realized reality.

Selection here does not mean that an external designer selects the candidate.It is a process of removing incompatible relationships, leaving behind a preserveable structure, and updating a consistent state within limited resources.In reality, it may be a process of implementing necessary relationships at the moment they are needed, rather than a list of all answers calculated and stored in advance.

*R = Π_constraints(S_possible)* (2.1)

The key question is not what should be counted, but what should not be counted.Reality can operate at a much lower representational cost if the necessary consistency and conservation can be maintained at the boundaries, without having to realize the details of every possible path independently.

*C_realized ≪ C_enumerated* (2.2)

Therefore, the minimal computation does not mean a lazy universe, but a universe that avoids duplication.Rather than creating the same relationship multiple times independently, multiple phenomena are organized together through common transmission and constraints.Subsequent dimensional filter and common carrier research attempted to translate this compression into a physical structure.

# Chapter 10 Minimal Computing Cosmology

The first principle of Minimal Computing Cosmology is that there is no reason for reality to classically determine and store all unobserved details.What is needed is not a state of nothing, but consistent rules between possible states and actual records.Therefore, the object of minimization includes not only the calculation method, but also the size of the source data, the order of reading the data, time evolution, and reality reproduction conditions.

C_min = min\[L_source + C_read + C_evolution + C_render + C_record\] (2.3)

The third principle is locality.If a small change in one point causes all technology in the entire universe to be rewritten from scratch, it is difficult for it to become a stable law of physics.Reality is updated through local interactions and limited transfers, and relationships in distant areas must be linked under common rules.

The fourth principle is reuse.If the mass, charge, spin, and position are made separately in four factories that are unrelated to each other and then attached to one particle, the binding rules explode.Explanation costs are reduced if multiple observations are jointly revealed in a common state and common transfer structure.

*ΔR(x) → ΔR(N_local(x))* (2.4)

In this view, laws are not complete scripts of all events, but compressed rules that limit the states that can be implemented.The initial conditions, boundary conditions, vacuum, and coupling constants specify one universe instance.Inputting observations is natural, but we must distinguish between input, structural derivation, and new predictions.
