# Chapter 53 Our universe is a possibility

## Multiple possible universes and our one universe

The two types of revenge should not be mixed here.It is one thing to say that multiple models of the universe with different laws, constants, sources, and renderers are feasible, and it is another claim to say that our universe continues to branch into real worlds at each moment of choice.

many realizable models ≠ many simultaneously actual histories (6.13)
