## Abstract
The proposal starts with a finite natural-number SOURCE and a folding-based classification of physical phenotypes. Primes correspond to an unfolded 1D layer; even composites to residue-free 2D folding; odd composites to a persistent residual unit, assigned to 3D matter or antimatter according to whether the remaining unit is attached in parallel to the upper (UP) or lower (DOWN) row. Conjugate UP/DOWN pairs cancel the signed residue and are hypothesized to change the output channel into light. The fourth dimension is the ordered rendering of phenotypes, rather than an additional fold; mathematical dimensions above four are interpreted as information layers. Conserved information changes phenotype. All physical mappings are hypotheses unless separately implemented and verified.

## UP/DOWN parallel folding
For odd composite 9, pair eight units into two parallel rows of four. The ninth unit remains **attached at the end of the upper row** (UP) or **at the end of the lower row** (DOWN); it is not detached above or below the structure. A candidate symbolic map F(n,σ)=(B(n),r(n,σ)) takes r(n,UP)=+1 and r(n,DOWN)=-1. Conjugate pairing gives r_total=0. A physical photon-output operator must still be defined; arithmetic cancellation does not itself imply electron–positron annihilation.

## Matter bias, annihilation, and mass–energy
The early renderer is hypothesized to favor one orientation, yielding matter dominance. This is not yet a computed baryogenesis model. A 3D UP + 3D DOWN pair is proposed to convert into a 2D light phenotype. Actual electron–positron annihilation at rest commonly yields two photons totaling about 1.022 MeV; photon multiplicity, polarization, energy–momentum, charge and Lorentz covariance remain required checks. E=mc² is a verification input, not a formula derived from folding.

## Information, quantization, time
Underlying information is hypothesized to be conserved while the observable phenotype changes. Let S_k be the full state, P_k=π(S_k) its phenotype, and S_(k+1)=T(S_k,e_k). Reversibility or auxiliary bookkeeping must preserve information in T. Rendering order produces experienced time; the shutter interval 1.4813×10^-21 s is a conditional MCC scale, not a proven universal clock. A strict spatial tunneling cutoff and accumulated quantization error remain separate OPEN hypotheses.

## Minimal computation
The guiding engineering preference is to prioritize architectures that reproduce the same reality with fewer independent resources and less redundant updating. This is a search prior, not a claim that nature necessarily minimizes computation. String-theoretic extra dimensions, QFT vacuum energy, and simulation costs raise different issues; MCC must match their verified outputs through its own transformations.
